Our Story

Making Tennis Accessible to Every Kid

The Match Point Project is a student-led initiative dedicated to making tennis accessible to every kid. Our mission is to give back to our community by collecting spare tennis gear.

As passionate tennis players ourselves, we know firsthand the joy this sport brings — and we believe every kid (no matter their background) deserves that same chance to play.

We strive to ensure the next generation will continue to be able to participate in tennis at the highest level. We aim to ensure no child is left on the sidelines due to the cost of equipment.

We're building partnerships with local courts, community centers, and youth organizations — including groups like the Boys & Girls Club — to get donated racquets directly into the hands of kids who can use them, wherever they're playing.

Frequently Asked Questions

Contact The Match Point Project at thematchpointproject.contact@gmail.com. We accept used racquets, dead or used tennis balls, and other gear in any condition. We clean and redistribute everything directly to young players who can't afford equipment, through the community partners and local courts we work with.

The Match Point Project is a student-led community initiative started by students at Leigh High School. We collect donated tennis gear to make the sport accessible to every child, regardless of their financial situation.

A beginner tennis racquet costs $30-$100, and court fees, lessons, and balls add up quickly. For many families, this cost puts tennis out of reach — which is exactly why The Match Point Project collects and donates gear for free.

We collect used racquets, balls, and tennis equipment, clean them up, and give them directly to kids who need them, including through the community partners we're building relationships with. No donation is too small — every piece of gear keeps a child on the court.

Yes! We happily accept dead tennis balls. We plan to distribute them to places in need and find other creative uses for them so they stay out of landfills. Every ball counts — reach out at thematchpointproject.contact@gmail.com to arrange a donation.

Our Mission Statement

The Match Point Project is a student-led initiative from Leigh High School dedicated to making tennis accessible to every kid. Our mission is to give back to our community by collecting spare tennis gear.

As passionate tennis players ourselves, we know firsthand the joy this sport brings — and we believe every kid (no matter their background) deserves that same chance to play.

We strive to ensure the next generation will continue to be able to participate in tennis at the highest level. We aim to ensure no child is left on the sidelines due to the cost of equipment.

By the end of the summer, we hope to have partnerships in place with local organizations — such as the Boys & Girls Club — to help distribute these donations and support youth tennis programs across our community.
